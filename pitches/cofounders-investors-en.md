# Elevator Pitch — Co-founders & Investors
## Vehicle Identity & Secondary Market Trust Layer

---

### 30-Second Version

The used car market is one of the largest transaction categories in the world — and one of the least trusted. Buyers can't verify what they're buying. Sellers can't prove what they're selling. The result: quality vehicles are undervalued, bad vehicles are overpriced, and everyone loses.

We're building the trust infrastructure that fixes this. Starting with a tool that independent vehicle inspectors actually want to use — a professional report generator that's better than what they have today. Every inspection they do creates a verified, signed event on a vehicle's permanent record. As these records accumulate across thousands of vehicles, we become the authoritative source of verified vehicle condition data.

The business model has three revenue layers: SaaS subscriptions for the professionals who use the tool, pay-per-query vehicle history reports for buyers and dealers, and institutional API access for insurers, lenders, and marketplaces. The pay-per-query model alone could generate $3-6M/year in Argentina at scale.

We start in LATAM, where the information gap is widest and verification carries the highest relative premium. The moat is the network — building a nationwide base of verified inspectors and workshops takes years and cannot be replicated by scraping public records.

### 60-Second Version (adds detail)

**The problem:** 2 million used vehicles change hands in Argentina every year. Buyers make high-value decisions on information they can't verify. Service history is fragmented across paper invoices and workshop memory. There's no Carfax. The closest equivalent — DataCar — offers basic institutional data (title, fines) but nothing about actual vehicle condition. The market systematically misprices risk.

**The insight:** Every inspection, every workshop visit, every diagnostic measurement produces real information about a vehicle. This data is created by professionals who see the vehicle directly — but it disappears into unverifiable PDFs and paper. If we capture these events in a structured, signed format as they happen, we build forward-looking vehicle identity — not a backward-looking report.

**The entry point:** We don't start by asking workshops to change their workflow. We start with independent vehicle inspectors and give them a genuinely better tool for the work they already do. Professional report generator, white-labeled with their brand, permanent verifiable links, accumulated public profile. They adopt it because it's a superior tool — signing and data accumulation are side effects.

**The business:** Revenue is zero in Phase 1 by design — the tool is free to maximize adoption. Phase 2 activates three revenue layers: (1) SaaS subscriptions for inspectors and workshops, (2) pay-per-query vehicle history reports with dynamic pricing based on data richness — including an inspection report upsell with revenue share to the inspector, and (3) institutional API access for insurers and lenders. The competitive moat is the verified network — it takes years to build and cannot be replicated.

**What we're validating now:** A 90-day plan with defined thresholds and kill criteria. Do inspectors perceive the tool as a genuine upgrade? Does usage persist? Do verified links generate buyer engagement? If the behavioral foundations aren't there, we stop before deploying significant capital.
