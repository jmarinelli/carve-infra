# Elevator Pitch — Inspectors (Phase 1)
## Vehicle Identity & Secondary Market Trust Layer

---

### 30-Second Version

You do inspections every day — and you probably deliver them as a PDF or a Word document that you built yourself. It works, but it's not ideal.

We built a tool that lets you generate professional inspection reports in less time. You define your own template — sections, checklists, whatever structure you use. You fill it in during the inspection with observations and photos. The tool generates a white-label report with your brand, your name, your identity. Not ours.

Each report gets a permanent link that renders beautifully when shared on WhatsApp or marketplace listings — with a professional preview showing the vehicle, the score, your name. Your clients share it, it looks great, and it makes you look more professional than the competition.

It's free. No subscription, no catch. We're building this and we need inspectors who actually know the trade to help us shape it.

### 60-Second Version (adds detail)

**The problem you have today:** You do quality work, but your deliverable looks the same as the guy who bought a scanner on MercadoLibre last week. There's no structured way for a buyer to tell who's a serious inspector and who's improvising. You compete on word of mouth and price — not on visible, verifiable track record.

**What we built:** A professional inspection report generator designed specifically for vehicle inspectors.

- You define your own inspection template — sections, items, order, level of detail. It adapts to how you work, not the other way around.
- During the inspection, you fill in observations, take photos directly in the app, add notes. Everything is structured.
- When you're done, the tool generates a professional report with your brand. Not a generic template — your report, your way, but polished.
- Each report gets a permanent, verifiable link. When your client shares it in a listing or on WhatsApp, it shows a professional preview: vehicle photo, score, your name, verification badge. It stands out.
- Over time, your profile accumulates: number of inspections completed, how long you've been operating, detail level. This is visible to anyone who sees your reports. The more you use it, the harder you are to compete with.

**What's in it for us:** We're building vehicle identity infrastructure. Every inspection you do creates a verified record on that vehicle's history. Over time, vehicles accumulate verified events from multiple professionals — creating something that doesn't exist today: a real, verifiable vehicle history built from professional observations.

You're not a data entry clerk for our platform. You're a professional whose work product becomes more valuable because it's structured, signed, and permanent. The report is yours. The profile is yours. We're the infrastructure underneath.

**The deal:** It's free. We're in Phase 1 — building the tool and the initial network. We need experienced inspectors to shape the product. If you're interested, you'd be one of the first verified inspectors on the platform.
