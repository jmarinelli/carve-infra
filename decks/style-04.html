The Problem

Market Structure
The secondary vehicle market systematically misprices risk — penalizing every honest participant.

Buyers make high-value decisions using information they cannot verify. Sellers of well-maintained vehicles cannot credibly prove their care. Quality vehicles are undervalued. Poor vehicles are overpriced.

Akerlof described this in 1970. Fifty years later, it remains the operating condition of the secondary vehicle market — globally.

Akerlof, G. (1970) — "The Market for Lemons: Quality Uncertainty and the Market Mechanism"
