The Problem

Market Structure
The condition

The secondary vehicle market is one of the largest transaction categories globally — and one of the least trusted.

"A market that systematically misprices risk."

Buyers make high-value decisions using information they cannot verify. Quality vehicles are undervalued. Poor vehicles are overpriced.

Why it persists

Service history — the most direct evidence of vehicle condition — is fragmented, non-portable, and trivially easy to fabricate.

Existing solutions are retrospective snapshots. They aggregate what was already recorded elsewhere.

50 yrs
Akerlof's "lemons" problem — still unresolved
The gap

Existing solutions do not create new signal. They do not address the structural asymmetry — they document it.

The raw material for verification is produced continuously — every oil change, every repair, every measurement. It is simply unstructured and unverifiable.

The challenge is infrastructure, not behavioral change.
