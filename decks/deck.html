The Problem

Market Structure

The secondary vehicle market is one of the largest transaction categories globally — and one of the least trusted.

Buyers make high-value decisions using information they cannot verify. Sellers of well-maintained vehicles cannot credibly prove their care. Quality vehicles are undervalued, poor vehicles are overpriced — and honest participants are systematically penalized.

Akerlof's "Market for Lemons" — identified in 1970. Fifty years later, it remains the operating condition of the secondary vehicle market.

The Insight

Core Thesis

Trust must be built forward,
not reconstructed backward.

The Service Layer

The vehicle's service layer — workshops, maintenance providers, inspection centers — is where real information about condition is created.

Every oil change, repair, and measurement is a verifiable event. If these events are captured in a structured, signed, immutable format at the moment they occur, the result is a primary record — not a report assembled from secondary sources.

No New Behavior Required

The system does not invent new behavior. It structures behavior that already exists.

Workshops already execute services

Owners already maintain vehicles

The raw material exists — it's simply unstructured

Why Workshops Adopt

Supply Side

Workshops are the supply side. Critically, their incentive does not depend on buyers caring yet.

Structured Job Registry

Replaces paper logs and fragmented records with a signed, portable, client-facing record of every service performed.

Dispute Protection

Signed, timestamped records as evidence when work is questioned. A fundamentally stronger evidentiary position than paper or WhatsApp.

Digital Service Certificates

Exportable proof of professional work. Travels with the vehicle into client hands — building the workshop's reputation.

Professional Differentiation

In a market where quality signaling is difficult, verified history attached to every vehicle serviced becomes a competitive edge.

These are operational advantages from day one — justified by immediate utility, not speculative signal value. 05 / 11

The LATAM Opportunity

Initial Market

The initial market is LATAM. High informality is counterintuitively an advantage.

Where nothing is verified, credible verification carries higher relative value.

A quality-conscious segment — enthusiast vehicles, higher-value transactions — provides a high-visibility wedge. Rapidly growing digital marketplace adoption creates distribution channels.

Real Frictions

Workshop fragmentation & digital literacy gaps

Tax visibility concerns in informal operations

Relational transaction culture

Structural Advantages
Informality Premium

Higher relative value for verification in a market where nothing is verified.

Deliberate Wedge

Quality-oriented, digitally capable workshops where documentation has immediate operational value.

Platform Tailwinds

Growing secondary market platforms create fast distribution channels for the trust signal.
