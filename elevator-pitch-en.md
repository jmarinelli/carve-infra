# Elevator Pitch

## The Problem

The used vehicle market operates on a fundamental contradiction: buyers make high-value decisions using information they cannot verify. Service history exists, but it's fragmented, non-standardized, and trivially easy to fabricate. The result is a market that rewards opacity and penalizes honesty — well-maintained vehicles sell at the same price as neglected ones.

## How It Works

We're building a vehicle identity layer from the service level up. Verified workshops digitally sign each service event — oil changes, repairs, inspections — generating an immutable, cumulative record tied to the vehicle's VIN. The output is a verification coverage signal that measures how much of a vehicle's lifecycle is backed by events signed by independent, accountable nodes.

The system requires no new behavior: workshops already perform the services. We structure what already happens and make it legible as a trust signal.

## Objectives

**Near-term (0–2 years):** Onboard quality workshops as verified nodes in LATAM, accumulate signed events, and validate that the adoption model works before committing to full infrastructure build.

**Mid-term (2–5 years):** Get the Official Vehicle Report into real transactions — listings, negotiations, financing — and establish verified coverage as a recognized input in price discussions between buyers and sellers.

**Long-term (5–10 years):** Enable insurers, lenders, and fleet operators to incorporate the signal into their risk models. The end state is a market where the absence of verified history carries a visible economic cost — and where a strong coverage record is a tradeable asset.
